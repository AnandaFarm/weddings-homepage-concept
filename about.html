Ananda Farm Weddings sits on Ananda Farm, a working forty-acre fruit and flower farm on Terra Ceia Island. The flower field your ceremony happens in is the same one the farm plants every season.

This page is almost entirely placeholder, and it is the one page I cannot write for you. The live /about-us/ page is empty, and an About page is where a family-run venue either earns trust or reads like every other listing.

What belongs here, roughly in order of value: why you started hosting weddings, who Jen and Chris are, a photograph of the two of you, what the farm was before it was a venue, and what you want a couple’s day to feel like. Two or three honest paragraphs in your own words will beat anything I could draft.

Your brand voice guide also has a specific rule here worth knowing: content about Kristy Boss needs ownership approval before publication, and her story is not a marketing device. If the farm’s origin belongs on this page, that approval comes first.
