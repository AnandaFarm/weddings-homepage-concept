The Wedding Experience at Ananda

A setting designed to flow naturally, from ceremony to celebration.

Schedule a Tour

Your wedding at Ananda is not built around a fixed format. It is shaped around your vision, your timeline, and the experience you want to create.

The property allows each part of the day to feel connected, relaxed, and intentional.

The shape of the day

How Your Wedding Day Comes Together

One

Ceremony

Start your day in a natural, open setting surrounded by mature trees and wide space. The ceremony area feels private and grounded, without distractions.

Two

Cocktail Hour

After the ceremony, guests move naturally into a cocktail hour set across the property. This transition feels effortless, with space to gather, move, and enjoy the setting.

Three

Reception

As the evening builds, the reception becomes the focal point, designed around your layout, your style, and your energy. Whether relaxed or high-energy, the space adapts to your vision.
