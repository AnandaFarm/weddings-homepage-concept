The property

The Property at Ananda

A private setting with multiple spaces designed to work together seamlessly.

Schedule a Tour

Ananda is designed as a connected property, where each part of your wedding day flows naturally from one space to the next.

From ceremony to reception, every area offers flexibility without losing a sense of cohesion.

Ceremony

Ceremony Locations

Primary

Primary Ceremony Area

A natural, open setting surrounded by mature trees and wide space. This area creates a private and grounded atmosphere for your ceremony.

Alternate

Alternate Ceremony Options

The property allows for multiple ceremony setups depending on your vision, guest count, and layout preferences.

View Ceremony Examples
Reception

Reception Areas

An open space designed to accommodate a range of layouts, from formal seated dinners to more relaxed, flowing receptions.

Flexible Layout Options

The space can be structured around your guest count, style, and event flow. There are no fixed layouts or restrictions.

Between the two

Cocktail Hour & Transition Spaces

Spaces that connect the experience.

Between the ceremony and reception, the property offers natural areas for cocktail hour and guest movement. These transitions feel seamless and unforced, allowing guests to explore and engage with the setting.
