Ask Us Something Else
When did Ananda Farm begin hosting weddings?

Ananda Farm began hosting weddings after years of welcoming guests to the property for gatherings, celebrations, and special events. The venue was created to offer couples a peaceful and scenic setting for wedding weekends, ceremonies, and receptions.

What makes Ananda Farm different from other wedding venues?

Ananda Farm offers a private and relaxed wedding experience surrounded by natural beauty. Couples can enjoy open outdoor spaces, a quiet countryside atmosphere, and the flexibility to create a celebration that feels personal and intentional.

Can couples schedule a tour of Ananda Farm before booking?

Yes. Couples are encouraged to schedule a private tour to walk the property, view ceremony and reception areas, and discuss wedding plans with the venue team. Touring the property helps couples better understand the layout, guest experience, and overall atmosphere of the venue.

What types of wedding ceremonies can be held at Ananda Farm?

Ananda Farm welcomes a wide range of wedding styles, including outdoor ceremonies, intimate gatherings, destination weddings, and full weekend celebrations. The property works well for couples looking for a natural and relaxed atmosphere.

How many guests can Ananda Farm accommodate?

Guest capacity can vary depending on the event layout, ceremony location, and reception setup. Couples should contact the venue directly to discuss guest counts and seating arrangements for their specific wedding plans.

Does Ananda Farm allow outside vendors?

Many couples choose to bring in their own preferred vendors for catering, photography, floral design, music, and rentals. Bar service is provided in house. Vendor policies and recommendations can be discussed during the planning process.

Is the property available for engagement photos or rehearsal events?

Yes. Many couples use the property for engagement sessions, rehearsal dinners, welcome gatherings, or other wedding weekend events. The natural scenery and open spaces create a strong backdrop for photography and guest experiences.

What time of year is best for a wedding at Ananda Farm?

Each season offers a different atmosphere at the property. Spring and fall are often popular for mild weather and outdoor ceremonies, while summer weddings can take advantage of longer daylight hours and evening celebrations.

Is parking available on-site for guests?

Yes. On-site parking is available for wedding guests and vendors. Details about parking flow and accommodations can be reviewed during event planning.

Can Ananda Farm host small weddings or elopements?

Yes. The venue can work well for both larger celebrations and smaller intimate weddings. Couples planning micro weddings or private ceremonies can discuss custom options with the venue team.
